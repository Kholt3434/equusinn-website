## Color Combinations & Usage Guidelines

### Primary Combination
- **Background:** Ivory (#FAF7F2)
- **Text:** Deep Navy (#1C2B4A)
- **Accents:** Champagne Gold (#D4AF6A)
- **Usage:** Main content areas, standard pages

### Premium/Luxury Combination
- **Background:** White (#FFFFFF)
- **Text:** Deep Navy (#1C2B4A)
- **Accents:** Champagne Gold (#D4AF6A)
- **Usage:** Cards, premium sections, forms

### Dark/Hero Combination
- **Background:** Deep Navy (#1C2B4A)
- **Text:** Ivory (#FAF7F2) or White (#FFFFFF)
- **Accents:** Champagne Gold (#D4AF6A)
- **Usage:** Hero sections, navigation, footers

### Warm Accent Combination
- **Background:** Ivory (#FAF7F2)
- **Text:** Deep Navy (#1C2B4A)
- **Accents:** Saddle Brown (#8B5E3C)
- **Usage:** Secondary sections, equestrian-themed areas

## Accessibility Notes

- **Contrast Ratio (Deep Navy on Ivory):** 9.2:1 (AAA compliant)
- **Contrast Ratio (Champagne Gold on Deep Navy):** 4.8:1 (AA compliant)
- **Recommended:** Use Deep Navy for all body text and primary headings
- **Champagne Gold:** Best used for highlights and non-critical accent text

## Design System Notes

- **Font Colors:** Deep Navy (#1C2B4A) for primary text, Dark Gray (#2A2A2A) for body
- **Link Colors:** Champagne Gold (#D4AF6A) for primary links
- **Border Colors:** Lunar Eclipse (#3D5A6B) or Light Gray borders
- **Button Colors:** Champagne Gold (#D4AF6A) background with Deep Navy (#1C2B4A) text
- **Hover States:** Slightly darker champagne or navy depending on context

## Brand Identity

These colors represent the Equus Inn brand:
- **Luxury & Sophistication:** Deep Navy, Champagne Gold
- **Equestrian Heritage:** Saddle Brown, warm earth tones
- **Comfort & Hospitality:** Ivory, warm neutrals
- **Professionalism:** Clean contrast ratios, consistent application
